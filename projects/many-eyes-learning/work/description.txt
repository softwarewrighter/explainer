What if your AI could learn from experiences it never had? When rewards are rare, a single explorer often misses them entirely. Many Eyes Learning solves this with multiple scouts sharing their discoveries.

In this video:
- The sparse reward problem in reinforcement learning
- How multiple scouts with different strategies cover more ground
- Architecture: scouts explore, experiences pool, DQN learns
- Live demo: 5 scouts achieving 94% success
- Scaling up: 9 scouts on a 6x6 grid with 93% success
- Why diversity beats repetition in exploration

Scout strategies shown:
- Random scouts: explore everywhere
- Epsilon-greedy scouts: balance exploration and exploitation
- Curious scouts: seek novel states
- Optimistic scouts: assume unknown areas have high value

The results speak for themselves. A single scout got zero percent success on this task. Five scouts sharing experiences jumped to sixty percent.
